# Salvador Dali inspired image

Use the user's subject and intended use as the content brief. Apply the visual treatment below without importing details from previous image subjects. Usually make a wide landscape editorial or blog-hero image; follow any aspect ratio or format the user requests.

## Shared visual treatment

- Keep the image minimal and art-directed: one clear focal idea, a few purposeful elements, generous open space, and a calm, coherent background. Avoid clutter and unnecessary decorative detail.
- Aim for sophisticated editorial art or art-book quality. Use precise painterly detail, delicate realism, clean forms, subtle grain, and beautiful atmospheric light. Keep surfaces and edges controlled rather than rough or chunky.
- Favor a spacious, still composition with a soft horizon when it suits the concept. Use painterly chiaroscuro and elongated shadows to add depth and quiet drama.
- Choose a restrained palette that supports the subject. Good starting points are near-black navy, warm ivory, and one muted electric-blue accent, or muted charcoal, parchment, and dusty blue. Keep accents sparse and colors harmonious; adapt them when the user specifies a palette.
- Keep the mood poetic, psychological, contemplative, and quietly uncanny. It can be quietly hopeful or unsettling, but should not become frightening, gory, loud, or epic unless requested.

## Surrealist treatment

When surrealism suits the request, draw on classic 20th-century dreamlike surrealism: precise, delicate realism combined with uncanny scale, perspective, shadow, or physical transformation. Make a requested transformation direct and readable in the form itself; do not replace it with an unrelated scene or add surrealist symbols by habit. Keep the surrealism elegant and sparse.

Treat an artist reference as guidance for visual treatment only. Do not depict or make characters resemble the artist, and do not add recognizable signature motifs unless the user asks for them. Do not reproduce a specific existing painting.

When the user gives enough information, generate the image directly with the available image-generation tool. Respect requested text, palette, composition, and format; otherwise, do not add words or logos.
